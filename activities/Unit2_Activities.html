Core message · Unit 2
Evidence is what the market tells you once you stop asking it to agree with you.
  • Customer conversations produce encouragement by default and evidence only by design.
  • Asking about past behaviour rather than future intention is what turns a conversation into data.
  • A validated venture is one whose assumptions survived a genuine attempt to kill them.

Driving question
What evidence would make you abandon your idea?
1
The False Positive
10 min

The facilitator reads three interview transcripts aloud. All three sound encouraging. All three are worthless.

Your task: identify what makes them worthless before the answer is given.

This is the failure mode of the entire unit — enthusiasm collected as if it were data.

2
Gallery Exploration
20 min

Open the Unit 2 Framework Gallery: Slibraries 3 and 4.

In pairs, select the framework you will use to structure your interviews and the one you will use to structure your competitive analysis.

Read the A4 sheet for The Mom Test before writing a single question.

3
Question Design
25 min

Write ten interview questions for your venture. Then delete every question that asks about the future or about your idea.

Rewrite what remains as questions about specific past behaviour: what did you do last time, what did it cost, what have you already tried?

Swap with another team and cut each other's leading questions without mercy.

4
Live Interviews
35 min

Interview real people: other students, staff, or contacts by phone. Two interviews minimum per team.

One person asks, one records verbatim. Do not summarise while listening.

Close every interview with a request for commitment — time, an introduction, or a small payment. A compliment is not a result.

Break · 10 minutes
5
Evidence Synthesis
30 min

Transfer your raw notes into an Empathy Map. Mark each entry with its source; leave empty what you did not observe.

Then use a retrieval-grounded AI research loop for the market context: retrieve sources first, generate second, and open every source it names.

Apply the Source Triangulation Check to any figure you intend to use in Assessment 1.

6
Competitive Position
25 min

Build a positioning map with two axes that are genuine buying criteria, taken from what your interviewees actually said.

Plot every alternative, including the workaround people use today and doing nothing.

State your position in one sentence you could defend to a competitor.

7
Kill or Continue
15 min

Each team states, in public, what its interviews disconfirmed. Teams that disconfirmed nothing present first.

Decide: continue, adjust the segment, or change the problem.

Complete the Unit 2 Reflection Prompt before the next session.

What to bring

  • Interview notebook — verbatim notes, not summaries
  • Phone for two live interviews
  • Empathy Map template
  • Access to Statista, Eurostat or the EUBS library
Deliverable by the end of the session: Two verbatim interview records, one Empathy Map built from them, and one positioning map with sourced axes.
