Driving question
What must be true for this plan to work?
1
The Assumption Hunt
10 min

Each team writes the single sentence that, if false, ends the venture.

Sentences go on the wall, unsigned. The room reads them.

Most will be assumptions the team has never tested. That is the working material for today.

2
Gallery Exploration
20 min

Open the Unit 4 Framework Gallery: Slibraries 7 and 8.

Read the A4 sheets for the Minto Pyramid Principle and the Risk Matrix.

Select the two frameworks you will use to structure your plan and your risk section.

3
Answer-First Drafting
30 min

Write your executive summary answer-first: the governing conclusion in sentence one, then three grouped supporting arguments, then evidence.

Test the groups against MECE — mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive.

Delete every sentence that builds towards the point rather than making it.

4
AI Critique Pass
30 min

Run the Prompt-to-Plan Workflow on your draft. Pass one: draft against an explicit brief. Pass two: ask the model to attack the draft against the marking criteria.

Pass three is yours alone — verify every factual claim it left in, and delete what you cannot source.

Pass four: citations and the AI declaration. Keep your prompts; they are part of the evidence trail.

Break · 10 minutes
5
Risk & Scenario Work
30 min

Build a risk matrix specific to your venture. Generic risks score zero today.

Every high-likelihood, high-impact risk gets an owner, a trigger and a written response.

Then build two scenarios from your most contested assumption, each with an early warning indicator.

6
Red Team
25 min

Teams exchange plans. Your job is to break the other team's plan in fifteen minutes: find the unsupported number, the missing competitor, the assumption dressed as a fact.

Report three findings to the room, in the pyramid structure: conclusion first.

Receiving teams may not defend during the report. Write everything down.

7
Repair List & Reflection
15 min

Convert the red-team findings into a prioritised repair list for Assessment 2.

Note which repairs need new evidence and which need only clearer writing.

Complete the Unit 4 Reflection Prompt before the next session.

What to bring

  • Draft executive summary (one page, written before the session)
  • AI tool with prompt history
  • Risk matrix template
  • Printed marking criteria for Assessment 2
Deliverable by the end of the session: An answer-first executive summary, a venture-specific risk matrix with owners and triggers, and a prioritised repair list.
