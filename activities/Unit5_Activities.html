Core message · Unit 5
Cash is a date, not a number — know the date, and know what moves it.
  • Profit is a statement about a period; cash is a fact about a day.
  • Ventures fail in the gap between when money leaves and when it arrives.
  • A forecast is credible only when every driver behind it can be defended on its own.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
3Analyse the stages of the entrepreneurial cycle.
4Demonstrate an understanding of the importance of research and careful planning when developing innovative business ventures.

Driving question
When does the money run out, and what changes that date?
1
The Runway Question
10 min

Each team states one number out loud: the date on which their venture runs out of money.

Teams that cannot state a date discover the point of the session immediately.

Write the date down. Everything today either moves it or fails to.

2
Gallery Exploration
20 min

Open the Unit 5 Framework Gallery: Slibraries 9 and 10.

Read the A4 sheets for the Three-Statement Model and Burn Rate & Runway.

Identify which framework you will use for the forecast and which for the funding section.

3
Assumptions Sheet
25 min

Build one assumptions sheet before touching any statement: price, volume drivers, conversion, cost per unit, headcount, timing.

Every driver carries a source or the word ASSUMED in capitals.

Hard-coded numbers inside formulas are an error, not a shortcut.

4
Three Statements
35 min

Drive the income statement from the assumptions sheet, roll the balance sheet forward, derive cash flow from the movement in balances.

Find your break-even volume and test it against your Unit 2 market sizing. Is it physically achievable?

Compute burn, runway and the date the raise must close.

Break · 10 minutes
5
Stress Test
25 min

Vary one driver at a time to find the two that dominate the outcome.

Build base, best and worst cases. Confirm the worst case is survivable, not merely unpleasant.

Use AI to check formulas and structure — not to invent inputs. Record what you asked it to check.

6
Funding Route
25 min

Place your venture on the Funding Ladder and state what evidence the next rung requires.

Model dilution across three rounds including the option pool. Compare offers on the value of your stake, not the percentage.

Teams considering crowdfunding: build the campaign funnel and identify who supplies the first third.

7
Number Defence
15 min

Each team defends one number under questioning for two minutes: where it came from and what would change it.

The room may not accept 'the model says so'.

Complete the Unit 5 Reflection Prompt before the next session.

What to bring

  • Spreadsheet with a separate assumptions sheet
  • Unit 2 market sizing and Unit 3 unit economics
  • AI tool used for formula checking only
  • Funding ladder worksheet
Deliverable by the end of the session: A linked three-statement model driven by one assumptions sheet, with break-even, runway date and a three-case sensitivity.
