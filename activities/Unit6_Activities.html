Core message · Unit 6
What you have not deliberately protected, you have already given away.
  • No legal system protects ideas; each regime protects a specific asset under specific conditions.
  • The expensive mistakes are irreversible acts taken in ignorance — a disclosure, a missing contract clause, an unregistered name.
  • Compliance is a design decision made before launch, not a document written after it.

Driving question
What do you own, and what can someone else take?
1
What Do You Actually Own?
10 min

Each team lists what its venture owns that a competitor could take.

Most lists will contain the word 'idea'. Ideas are not protectable.

Hold the list; by the end of the session every item will have a regime or a reason it has none.

2
Gallery Exploration
20 min

Open the Unit 6 Framework Gallery: Slibraries 11 and 12.

Read the A4 sheets for the IP Protection Matrix and the Patentability Filter.

Note the two decisions that cannot be reversed: public disclosure and choice of legal form.

3
Live Clearance Searches
30 min

Run a trade mark clearance search on your venture name at EUIPO and the WIPO Global Brand Database, in the classes you would actually trade in.

Technical ventures: run a prior-art search on Espacenet.

Screenshot what you find. A name that is not clear is cheaper to change today than after the launch spend.

4
Legal Form and IP Map
30 min

Work the legal form decision tree: liability, taxation, capital, governance, investor readiness.

Map every intangible asset onto a regime — patent, trade mark, copyright, design right, trade secret — and note what would destroy that protection.

Decide, explicitly, what you will keep secret and what you will publish.

Break · 10 minutes
5
Operations Design
25 min

Map your value chain and mark the two or three activities that create the differentiation you claim.

Build a supply chain risk radar including platform and API dependencies. Mark every single point of failure.

Define one PDCA loop you will run in the first ninety days after launch.

6
Compliance Pass
25 min

Classify every AI use in your venture against the EU AI Act risk tiers and determine whether you are provider, deployer or both.

For each processing of personal data, state the lawful basis under Article 6 before you collect anything.

Where you rely on legitimate interests, write the balancing test in three sentences.

7
Structure Sheet & Reflection
15 min

Produce one page: legal form, IP map, AI classification, data bases, key dependencies.

This page becomes the operational and legal section of Assessment 2.

Complete the Unit 6 Reflection Prompt before the next session.

What to bring

  • Laptop for EUIPO, Espacenet and WIPO searches
  • Legal form decision tree worksheet
  • IP protection matrix
  • EUR-Lex access for the AI Act and GDPR articles
Deliverable by the end of the session: A one-page structure sheet: legal form, IP map with regimes, AI Act classification and GDPR lawful bases.
