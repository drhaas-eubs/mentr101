Driving question
Why would a stranger buy this, this week?
1
The Stranger Test
10 min

Each team has thirty seconds to explain its offer to a person from another team who knows nothing about it.

The listener then repeats it back. What survives the repetition is your message; what does not, is not.

Write down what survived.

2
Gallery Exploration
20 min

Open the Unit 7 Framework Gallery: Slibraries 13 and 14.

Read the A4 sheets for STP and the SUCCESs checklist.

Choose the framework you will use for segmentation and the one you will use for the funnel.

3
Segment and Position
25 min

Apply segmentation, targeting and positioning using the evidence from Unit 2, not intuition.

Targeting is a decision to exclude: name the segments you are deliberately not serving.

Write your position as one sentence you could defend to a competitor's sales team.

4
Message Build and Test
30 min

Draft three versions of your core message and test each against the six SUCCESs properties.

Replace every abstraction with something the listener can picture.

Test on three people outside your team. Keep the version they can repeat.

Break · 10 minutes
5
Funnel and Pipeline
30 min

Build your acquisition funnel with a conversion assumption at each stage, and your sales pipeline with objective exit criteria.

Compute what a customer costs at your assumed conversion rates. Compare it with the contribution margin from Unit 3.

Identify the single weakest transition. That is the only one you may work on first.

6
AI in the Channel
25 min

Decide where AI belongs in your marketing: content, personalisation, targeting, service — and where it does not.

For each use, state the data it needs and the lawful basis for that data. Personalisation without a basis is not an option.

Apply the manipulation matrix to any engagement mechanic you are designing.

7
Go-to-Market Page & Reflection
15 min

Produce one page: segment, position, message, channels, funnel with conversion assumptions, and the first ninety days.

This becomes the marketing strategy section of Assessment 2.

Complete the Unit 7 Reflection Prompt before the next session.

What to bring

  • Unit 2 interview evidence
  • Unit 3 unit economics
  • Three willing strangers for message testing
  • Funnel template with conversion fields
Deliverable by the end of the session: A one-page go-to-market plan: segment, position, tested message, funnel with conversion assumptions and CAC.
