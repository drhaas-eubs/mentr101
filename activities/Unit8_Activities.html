Driving question
What would you have to build to still be right in three years?
1
The Three-Year Question
10 min

One question opens the session: what would you have to build for this venture to still be right in three years?

Two minutes of silence, then three answers from the room.

The answer belongs in your closing slide, not your opening one.

2
Gallery Exploration
20 min

Open the Unit 8 Framework Gallery: Slibraries 15 and 16.

Read the A4 sheets for Crossing the Chasm and the Greiner Growth Curve.

Select the framework that structures your launch and the one that structures your scaling section.

3
Launch and Scale Design
30 min

Define your beachhead segment and the whole product it requires. Narrow before you broaden.

Build the ninety-day go-to-market timeline: pre-launch, launch window, first thirty days, days thirty to ninety.

Name the growth crisis you expect first, using Greiner, and what structure would answer it.

4
Metrics and Responsibility
25 min

Set a North Star metric that measures value received by the customer and decompose it into three drivers.

Run the AI Scaling Readiness Check across data, talent, governance and process.

Complete the Trustworthy AI closing checklist and name the person accountable for each requirement.

Break · 10 minutes
5
Pitch Build
25 min

Assemble ten to twelve slides: why, problem and solution, market and validation, model, marketing and sales, financials, ask, close.

Structure answer-first. Test every slide against the SUCCESs checklist.

Attach your AI Use Declaration; it is part of the submission, not an afterthought.

6
Live Pitches and Q&A
35 min

Ten minutes per team, then five minutes of questions from the room and the facilitator.

Questions are drawn from your own plan: the unsupported number, the missing competitor, the assumption dressed as a fact.

You may not answer with 'the model says so'. This is the competence Assessment 2 examines.

7
Repair List & Course Close
15 min

Convert the questions you could not answer into the final repair list for Assessment 2.

Close your personal Slibrary: the frameworks you will actually carry out of this course.

Complete the Unit 8 Reflection Prompt — it is the course reflection.

What to bring

  • Draft pitch deck, 10-12 slides
  • Full financial model from Unit 5
  • AI Use Declaration draft
  • Timer — ten minutes is enforced
Deliverable by the end of the session: A rehearsed ten-minute pitch, a ninety-day launch timeline, a North Star metric with drivers, and the AI declaration.
