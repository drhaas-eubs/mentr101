This is the working environment for MENTR101. Every unit of the course outline has its own framework gallery built in two Slibraries: the established entrepreneurship frameworks, and a second Slibrary integrating the use of artificial intelligence at that stage of the entrepreneurial cycle. Every framework carries a professional definition, four working points and a Harvard reference.
