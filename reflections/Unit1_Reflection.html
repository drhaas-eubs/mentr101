Reflection
An idea becomes an opportunity only when it meets a real need with means you actually control.Core message after Bostelaar · Intended learning outcomes 1, 3

Complete these reflections before the next session. Bring your written responses — they open our discussion.

1
Which framework from today most changed how you think about your own idea? Describe the specific moment: what did you see differently after working with it?
2
You used AI to expand your ideas today. Look back at your prompts and outputs. Which of the generated variants would you have accepted without checking, and what would that have cost you?
3
Choose one framework from the Unit 1 gallery you do not yet fully understand. Open its A4 reference sheet, read the definition and the sources, and write a 200-word explanation in your own words. Bring it to the next session.
Independent study: these three responses are part of the 11 hours of individual study in the course workload.
