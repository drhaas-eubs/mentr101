Reflection
Evidence is what the market tells you once you stop asking it to agree with you.Core message after Bostelaar · Intended learning outcomes 2, 4

Complete these reflections before the next session. Bring your written responses — they open our discussion.

1
Your interviews today produced evidence and encouragement. Separate them: what did you learn that you did not want to hear?
2
Which of your venture's assumptions did the interviews weaken, and what is the cheapest test of the next one?
3
Take one market figure you intend to use in Assessment 1. Trace it to its original source and write down what you found. If it cannot be traced, say what you will use instead.
Independent study: these three responses are part of the 11 hours of individual study in the course workload.
