A working product is not a business; the model is how the value you create comes back as money.Core message after Bostelaar · Intended learning outcomes 1, 3

Complete these reflections before the next session. Bring your written responses — they open our discussion.

1
Which block of your canvas has the least evidence behind it, and what would it take to change that before Assessment 1?
2
You chose a revenue mechanism today. Write 150 words on what it commits you to — cash timing, churn exposure, forecasting method.
3
Open the A4 sheet for one framework in Slibrary 6 that you did not use today. Explain in your own words why it might matter to your venture later.
Independent study: these three responses are part of the 11 hours of individual study in the course workload.
