Reflection
A plan is worth what its weakest assumption can withstand.Core message after Bostelaar · Intended learning outcomes 3, 4

Complete these reflections before the next session. Bring your written responses — they open our discussion.

1
Write the one sentence that, if false, ends your venture. Then write what evidence would tell you it is false.
2
You ran an AI critique pass on your draft. Which of its criticisms did you reject, and were you right to?
3
Review your risk matrix. Which risk did you leave out because naming it was uncomfortable, and where does it belong?
Independent study: these three responses are part of the 11 hours of individual study in the course workload.
