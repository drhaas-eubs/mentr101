Reflection
Cash is a date, not a number — know the date, and know what moves it.Core message after Bostelaar · Intended learning outcomes 3, 4

Complete these reflections before the next session. Bring your written responses — they open our discussion.

1
State the date your venture runs out of money and the cheapest action that moves it by one month.
2
Which two assumptions dominate your forecast? Describe how you would test the weaker of the two in a week.
3
Look back at what you entered into AI tools today. Was any of it above the public tier? Write down the rule you will apply from now on.
Independent study: these three responses are part of the 11 hours of individual study in the course workload.
