Reflection
What you have not deliberately protected, you have already given away.Core message after Bostelaar · Intended learning outcomes 3, 4

Complete these reflections before the next session. Bring your written responses — they open our discussion.

1
List what your venture owns. For each item, name the regime that protects it and the act that would destroy that protection.
2
What has your venture already disclosed publicly that now limits your options? What will you disclose differently?
3
State your AI Act classification and your GDPR lawful basis in three sentences each, as they will appear in Assessment 2.
Independent study: these three responses are part of the 11 hours of individual study in the course workload.
