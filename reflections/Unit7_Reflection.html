The cheapest customer is the one who stays, not the one who clicks.Core message after Bostelaar · Intended learning outcomes 2, 3

Complete these reflections before the next session. Bring your written responses — they open our discussion.

1
Write your position in one sentence, then name the three segments you are deliberately not serving.
2
Which stage of your funnel is genuinely weakest? Describe the one experiment you would run on it next week.
3
Describe one personalisation or engagement mechanic you decided not to build, and the reason you rejected it.
Independent study: these three responses are part of the 11 hours of individual study in the course workload.
