Scaling is a decision you make deliberately, not a reward you receive for growing.Core message after Bostelaar · Intended learning outcomes 1, 2, 3

Complete these reflections before the next session. Bring your written responses — they open our discussion.

1
Which beachhead segment will you dominate first, and what does the whole product for that segment include?
2
Name the growth crisis you expect first and the structural change that would answer it before it arrives.
3
Course reflection: which three frameworks from the eighty leave this course with you, and how will you use each one in the next twelve months? Write 400 words.
Independent study: these three responses are part of the 11 hours of individual study in the course workload.
