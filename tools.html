Every tool below is mapped to the units where it earns its place. The caution line is not decoration: in this course a tool is only as good as the evidence discipline around it. Licensed databases are accessed through the EU Business School library.

⌕

Standing rule for every AI tool on this page

Confidential, personal or client data is never pasted into a public AI service. Every fact, figure and citation an AI tool produces is verified against its primary source before it enters a submission, and all AI use is declared.

Use in this course: Divergent idea generation, adversarial critique of your own plan, rewriting for clarity.
