Core message · Unit 1
An idea becomes an opportunity only when it meets a real need with means you actually control.
  • Ideas are abundant, and generating them is now effectively free.
  • What is scarce is the judgement that separates an idea from an opportunity for this team, at this moment, with these means.
  • Screening, not generating, is the entrepreneurial skill this unit builds.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
1Systematically analyse current and future business opportunities in terms of entrepreneurial behaviours and capability creation.
3Analyse the stages of the entrepreneurial cycle.
Core message distilled after Bostelaar (2024), Clarify Your Core Message: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.

Driving question

Is an opportunity discovered, or is it made?

Unit content (course outline, section 4)

  • Understanding Entrepreneurship: defining entrepreneurship, types of entrepreneurs, and the entrepreneurial mindset
  • Sources of Ideas: techniques for idea generation, recognising opportunities, and brainstorming
  • Idea Evaluation: criteria for evaluating ideas, feasibility analysis, and initial concept validation

AI integration in this unit

Students use generative AI as a divergence engine — expanding the option space — while judgement, screening and selection stay human. The unit establishes the course-wide rule: AI drafts, the entrepreneur decides, and every AI contribution is declared.
