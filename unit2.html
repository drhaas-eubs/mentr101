Core message · Unit 2
Evidence is what the market tells you once you stop asking it to agree with you.
  • Customer conversations produce encouragement by default and evidence only by design.
  • Asking about past behaviour rather than future intention is what turns a conversation into data.
  • A validated venture is one whose assumptions survived a genuine attempt to kill them.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
2Interpret trends, behaviours and changes in various market environments.
4Demonstrate an understanding of the importance of research and careful planning when developing innovative business ventures.

Driving question

What evidence would make you abandon your idea?

Unit content (course outline, section 4)

  • Market Analysis: identifying target markets, analysing industry trends, and understanding customer needs
  • Competitive Analysis: assessing competitors, defining unique selling propositions (USPs), and market positioning

AI integration in this unit

AI accelerates desk research and synthesis, but market evidence must be traceable. Students learn retrieval-based research, source triangulation and a hallucination check before any figure enters the Market Validation Report.
