Core message · Unit 4
A plan is worth what its weakest assumption can withstand.
  • The value of planning lies in the reasoning it forces, not in the document it produces.
  • A plan earns credibility through structure, verified evidence and honestly named risk.
  • Fluent prose is now free, so only the argument underneath it distinguishes a serious plan.

In-class activities

Two activities you run with the frameworks on this page. Each is timed, each ends in something you hand in, and each feeds the assessment. The full seven-phase session plan is in the session activities for this unit.

A1
Answer First: Rewrite Your Executive Summary
25 min

Frameworks: Minto Pyramid Principle (sheet 33) · Business Plan Architecture (sheet 32)  ·  Working format: Individually, then pairs

  1. Ten minutes: write your executive summary answer-first. Sentence one is the governing conclusion. Then three grouped arguments. Then the evidence under each.
  2. Five minutes: delete every sentence that appears before the conclusion. Context is not an introduction.
  3. Five minutes: test your three groups against MECE — do they overlap, and what falls outside all three?
  4. Five minutes: read only your first sentence to a partner. If they cannot say what the venture does and why it works, rewrite that sentence.

You hand in: An executive summary of at most 300 words, conclusion first.

Watch out: If the governing conclusion cannot be written yet, that is a finding about the plan, not about the writing.

A2
Three Passes on a Machine Draft
25 min

Frameworks: Prompt-to-Plan Workflow (sheet 37) · AI Use Declaration Protocol (sheet 38)  ·  Working format: Pairs

  1. Five minutes: pass one. Give a model an explicit brief — audience, length, evidence available — and have it draft one section of your plan. Keep the prompt.
  2. Five minutes: pass two. Ask the same model to attack the draft against the marking criteria. Keep what it finds.
  3. Ten minutes: pass three is yours alone. Take every factual claim, figure and citation the model produced and trace it to a source. Delete what you cannot trace — do not soften it.
  4. Five minutes: complete the AI Use Declaration for this section: tool, purpose, what you verified.

You hand in: One verified section, the prompts used, and a completed declaration.

Watch out: Count what survives pass three. That ratio is the honest measure of what the tool contributed.
