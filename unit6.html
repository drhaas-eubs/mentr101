Core message · Unit 6
What you have not deliberately protected, you have already given away.
  • No legal system protects ideas; each regime protects a specific asset under specific conditions.
  • The expensive mistakes are irreversible acts taken in ignorance — a disclosure, a missing contract clause, an unregistered name.
  • Compliance is a design decision made before launch, not a document written after it.

Driving question

What do you own, and what can someone else take?

Unit content (course outline, section 4)

  • Legal Considerations: business structures (sole proprietorship, partnership, LLC, corporation), licences, and permits
  • Operational Planning: setting up operations, supply chain management, and process optimisation
  • Intellectual Property: protecting your idea through patents, trademarks, and copyrights

AI integration in this unit

AI-generated output raises live questions of ownership and authorship, and AI deployment carries legal duties under the EU AI Act and GDPR. Students learn what they can own, what they must disclose, and what they must document.
