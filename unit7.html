Core message · Unit 7
The cheapest customer is the one who stays, not the one who clicks.
  • Acquisition is visible and purchasable; activation and retention decide whether buying it was worth anything.
  • Positioning is a decision about whom to exclude, and excluding badly fills the funnel with people you cannot serve.
  • Personalisation is limited by consent and trust, not by technology.

Unit content (course outline, section 4)

  • Marketing Fundamentals: creating a marketing plan, the 4 Ps of marketing, and branding strategies
  • Digital Marketing: social media, SEO, content marketing, and email campaigns
  • Sales Techniques: sales strategy, sales funnel management, and customer relationship management (CRM)

AI integration in this unit

AI now produces content, personalisation and outreach at scale. The unit teaches where that creates advantage, where it destroys trust, and what the transparency duty for AI-generated content requires.
